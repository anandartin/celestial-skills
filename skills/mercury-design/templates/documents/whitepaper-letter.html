1. Why now

The cost of coordination has quietly become the largest line in most operating budgets. This piece walks through how we got there: what we measured, what we removed, and the three habits that held once the novelty wore off.

We started by counting. Every hand-off, every re-typed field, every message that existed only to ask whether something had been done. The list was long and boring, which is exactly the point.

2. What we measured

Hand-offs, re-keyed data, and time-to-answer for the ten most common questions.
