01 / SUPPORT

Support that answers, not deflects.

Mars reads the whole thread, checks the account and replies with the specific answer — four in five conversations close without a hand-off.

GOGrace Okafor · Head of Design·September 2026·6 min read

Most teams do not need more tools; they need fewer decisions. Mars folds the front desk, the billing binder and the newsletter into one place your staff already knows how to use.

The result is not just speed. When the record of work lives in one system, the questions people ask change — from “where is it?” to “what should we do next?”.

Why now

The cost of coordination has quietly become the largest line in most operating budgets. This piece walks through how we got there: what we measured, what we removed, and the three habits that held once the novelty wore off.

“We replaced four tools and nobody asked for them back.”
Maya Reyes, Head of Operations, Northwind

What we measured

Hand-offs, re-keyed data, and time-to-answer for the ten most common questions. We started by counting. Every hand-off, every re-typed field, every message that existed only to ask whether something had been done. The list was long and boring, which is exactly the point.

Result81%resolved without a hand-off

What changed

One record per family, one inbox per team, and a weekly rhythm the software enforces.
